# show me the delay

a client side fabric mod for minecraft 1.21.11 that shows you the exact delay in milliseconds when you do things like placing blocks or attacking. its basically a ping check for your actions so you know if its lag or you just missed.

it adds a clean hud on your screen that tracks your recent actions and fades out over time. you can configure everything by pressing O in-game.

## features
- shows exact millisecond delay for block placing and attacking
- fully customizable hud (change opacity, history size, fade time, corner radius)
- drag and drop the hud anywhere on your screen in the config menu
- super optimized, works fine with optimization mods

## how to use
just drop it in your mods folder. 
press O in-game to open the configuration menu. you can drag the preview box around to place it wherever you want.
